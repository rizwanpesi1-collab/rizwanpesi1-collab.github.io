📚 CV Guide

How to make a good CV?

Use a clear name, contact information, education, work experience, skills and languages. Keep your CV simple, readable and professional.

What skills should students add?

Students can add communication, teamwork, Microsoft Office, computer skills, problem solving, time management and other relevant skills.

What should freshers write?

Freshers can focus on education, projects, internships, certifications, skills and career objectives.
