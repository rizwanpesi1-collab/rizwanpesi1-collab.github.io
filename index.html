📚 How to Make a Professional CV

A professional CV should clearly present your education, experience, skills and contact information. The most important thing is that the information is accurate, easy to read and relevant to the job you are applying for.

1. Add Your Personal Information

Start with your full name, email address, phone number and location or address. Use an email address that looks professional and make sure your contact information is correct.

2. Write a Professional Summary

A professional summary is a short introduction that explains your background, strengths and career goals. Keep it focused on the type of work you are applying for.

3. Add Your Education

List your latest or most relevant qualification first. Include the qualification, institution and year or result when useful. Students and freshers can give more space to education when they have limited work experience.

4. Add Work Experience

For each job, include the company, job title and important responsibilities. Focus on relevant work and describe what you actually did rather than adding information that is not accurate.

5. Add Relevant Skills

Skills should match your real abilities and, when possible, the requirements of the job. Examples can include communication, MS Office, data entry, typing, teamwork and time management. Only list skills you genuinely have.

6. Add Languages

List languages you can communicate in. If appropriate, you can also mention your level, such as basic, intermediate or fluent.

7. Choose the Right CV Template

Choose a CV design that makes your information easy to read. For many office and professional applications, a clean and structured layout can make the information easier to scan. CV PRO AI provides 13 template options.

8. Download Your CV as PDF

After checking your information, use the Save CV as PDF button to create a PDF version. Before sending the CV to an employer, open the PDF and check that the text, spacing and contact information appear correctly.

9. Check Your CV Before Applying

Check spelling, phone number, email address, education, experience and skills before submitting your CV. Make sure your information is truthful and relevant to the position.

CV Tips for Students and Freshers

If you are a student or fresher and do not have much work experience, focus on education, projects, computer skills, communication skills, certifications, internships and other relevant achievements that you actually have.

Common CV Mistakes

Common problems include spelling mistakes, incorrect contact details, unnecessary information, very long paragraphs, unprofessional email addresses and claiming skills or experience that you do not have.
